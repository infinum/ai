# User Interview Question Library

A reference bank of questions organised by section type. Pick and adapt — never use all of them. Every section can be followed up with "Why?", "Can you tell me more about that?", or "Do you have a recent example?"

---

## Warm-Up / Intro (always include a few of these)

- Could you start by telling me a little bit about yourself? How do you spend your time?
- Where do you live?
- Who do you live with / who do you spend your time with?
- How tech savvy would you say you are? How do you access the internet?
- (If relevant) Tell me about [your child / your pet / your property / your business].

---

## Background & Context

- How did you first hear about / come across [topic / product]?
- What led you to look into [topic] in the first place?
- Have you looked into this before, or is this your first time?
- What has the process of searching / researching been like so far?
- How did you feel when you first started looking into this?
- What methods have you used to find / research [topic]?
- Do you typically search via phone, laptop, or something else?
- What words or phrases did you search for?
- What kind of results did you find? Did they meet your expectations?

---

## Goals & Motivations

- What are the most important factors to you when it comes to [topic]?
- When did you first decide you wanted to look into [topic]?
- What are you ultimately hoping to accomplish?
- What does success look like to you here?
- Do you have an idea of what type of [service / product] you're looking for, or are you unsure and looking for guidance?
- Do you prefer to do a lot of research before moving forward, or do you want to get started as quickly as possible?

---

## Pain Points & Challenges

- Have you come across any challenges or deterrents in your search process?
- What has been most confusing or frustrating?
- What would make you immediately leave a site and look somewhere else?
- Have you come up with any workarounds to solve a challenge?
- What are the biggest questions you've had as you've gone through this process?

---

## Website / Product Expectations

- When you land on a [provider's / company's] website, what do you look for first?
- What information do you absolutely need before you'd consider reaching out?
- What features or information do you expect to see on the site?
- What makes a website feel trustworthy to you?
- Did any site feel like it was really speaking to you — what did it say or show?
- Is there information you'd want to see that most sites don't seem to provide?
- How do you prefer to consume content online? (Long articles, quick summaries, videos, etc.)
- Is it important to you that the provider have educational content (articles, condition info) that you can read?

---

## Trust & Decision-Making

- What's the #1 deciding factor in choosing [a provider / product / service]?
- What would be a "green flag" — a sign this is the right fit? What would be a "red flag"?
- What would make you feel confident enough to take the next step (book, buy, sign up)?
- What do you need to understand or feel reassured about before committing?
- How much research do you typically do before making this kind of decision? How does it compare to similar decisions?
- Do you have a go-to source for this type of [product / service]? Why them?

---

## Conversion & Next Steps

- Would you prefer to [book / contact / sign up] online or call? Why?
- How soon are you hoping to get the process started?
- When you take the next step, what do you expect to happen?
- What would prevent you from completing that step?

---

## Comparison & Competition

- What other [providers / products / options] have you considered?
- How did those compare — what did they do better? What did they not do as well?
- Have you browsed or purchased from any other companies in this space?
- What are the deciding factors on how you choose who to go with?

---

## Product / App Specific (for usability or product-feature research)

- What are your first impressions of this [site / app / product]?
- What do you think this is for?
- Is this helpful to you? What other information would you like to see here?
- What do you think is working well, and where do you think it's lacking?
- What features would you use the most? Why?
- Which features would you not use? Why?
- What features don't exist that you'd like to see?
- What do you think of the overall design / branding?
- How much would you be willing to pay for something like this?

---

## Ideas & Vision

- What do you wish was different about [topic / existing product]?
- Are there any apps or services you use that you think do a good job addressing the challenges we've talked about — even in a different domain?
- If you had a magic wand and could build your dream [product / solution], what would that look like?

---

## Wrap-Up (always end with these)

- Is there anything we haven't covered yet that you think we should know?

---

## Probing / Follow-Up Prompts (use freely throughout)

- Can you tell me a bit more about that?
- Do you have a recent example of that?
- How did that experience make you feel?
- What else? Do you have any more examples?
- Why is that important to you?
- What would that look like in practice?
