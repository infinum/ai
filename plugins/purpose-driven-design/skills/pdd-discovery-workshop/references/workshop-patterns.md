# Workshop Patterns — mapping, sequencing & time budgeting

How to turn goals into a workshop plan. Use with `activity-library.md`.

---

## Step A — Map goals → activity groups

Identify the 1–2 groups that serve the stated goals, then pick activities from those groups.

| If the goal is… | Lead group | Good activities |
|---|---|---|
| Align on vision / what success looks like | Context & Alignment | Extra Extra! → Elevator Pitch **or** Press Release; Goals, Signals & Metrics |
| Understand who we're building for | Users | Proto-Persona Sketch; Empathy / Day-in-the-Life; JTBD |
| Generate ideas / creative direction | Ideation & Design | Crazy 8s; Lightning Demos; How Might We |
| Decide what to focus on / scope | Prioritisation | Value–Effort Matrix **or** Buy a Feature; Dot-Voting |
| De-risk / surface concerns | Reflection (+ Context) | Stakeholder & Landscape Map; Rose/Bud/Thorn |

Pick **one** warm-up plus **1–2** longer exercises. Don't run two activities that do the same
job (e.g. Elevator Pitch *and* Press Release; Value–Effort *and* Buy a Feature).

---

## Step B — Sequence

Order the chosen activities by these rules:

1. **Warm-up first** — always, to settle the room and teach board mechanics.
2. **Diverge before converge** — generate (Crazy 8s, brainstorm) before you narrow (dot-vote,
   Value–Effort).
3. **Context → Users → Design → Prioritise** — move from framing outward to specifics.
4. **Reflection last** — Rose/Bud/Thorn or a quick retro closes the session.
5. **Protect the highest-value exercise** — put it early enough that a slow start doesn't eat it.

---

## Step C — Budget the time

Workshops always run long. Budget with overhead, not just activity times.

**Fixed overhead (per session):**
- Intro / framing: **5 min**
- Transitions between activities: **~3 min each**
- Break: **5–10 min** (only for sessions ≥ 90 min)
- Wrap-up / next steps: **5 min**
- Buffer: **~10% of total** (round up)

**Formula:** `sum(activity midpoints) + 5 (intro) + 3×(transitions) + break + 5 (wrap) + 10% buffer`
→ must be **≤ available time**. If it overflows, drop the lowest-value longer exercise or shorten
depth; if it underflows, add depth or a second longer exercise (not a redundant one).

**Rule of thumb for number of longer exercises:** 60 min = 1 · 90 min = 1–2 · 2 hrs = 2 ·
(3 only for a rare half/full day).

## Ready-made agendas (starting points — adjust to goals)

### 60 minutes — one focused outcome
| # | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up (Either/Or or Two Truths) | 5 |
| 2 | Intro & goals | 5 |
| 3 | One longer exercise (e.g. Elevator Pitch **or** Value–Effort) | 35 |
| 4 | Debrief + Rose/Bud/Thorn | 10 |
| 5 | Wrap & next steps | 5 |

### 90 minutes — align + one deeper thread
| # | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up (Extra, Extra!) | 8 |
| 2 | Intro & goals | 5 |
| 3 | Context: Elevator Pitch **or** Goals/Signals/Metrics | 20 |
| 4 | Break | 5 |
| 5 | Users **or** Ideation: Proto-Persona / Crazy 8s / HMW | 30 |
| 6 | Prioritise: Dot-vote or Value–Effort (light) | 12 |
| 7 | Wrap & next steps | 5 |
| — | Buffer | ~5 |

### 2 hours — vision → users → prioritise
| # | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up (Extra, Extra!) | 8 |
| 2 | Intro & goals | 5 |
| 3 | Context: Press Release **or** Elevator Pitch | 25 |
| 4 | Users: Proto-Persona / Empathy | 30 |
| 5 | Break | 10 |
| 6 | Prioritise: Value–Effort **or** Buy a Feature | 25 |
| 7 | Reflection: Rose/Bud/Thorn | 12 |
| 8 | Wrap & next steps | 5 |
| — | Buffer | ~10 |

## After the workshop (synthesis path)
Point the team at the next PDD steps depending on what the session produced:
- Raw notes/stickies → **affinity mapping** and, from interview data, **`pdd-rainbow-analysis`** / **`pdd-interview-summary`** / **`pdd-archetypes`**.
- Agreed goals/scope → **`pdd-project-brief`**, **`pdd-estimate`**, **`pdd-schedule`**.
- Concerns/risks surfaced → **`pdd-risk-radar`**.
- A "Discovery Readout" consolidating findings.
