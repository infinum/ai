# Discovery Activity Library

The menu of activities for discovery workshops. Each entry has a **time band**, the **goal
group** it serves, its **purpose**, **how to run it**, the **output**, and **selection notes**.
Pick a warm-up + 1–2 longer exercises to fit the session (see `workshop-patterns.md`). Times are
the range for a typical 4–8 person group; use the midpoint when budgeting.

Groups: **Context & Alignment**, **Users**, **Ideation & Design**, **Prioritisation**,
**Reflection**. Warm-ups are separate.

---

## Warm-ups (pick exactly one, always first — 5–10 min)

### Extra, Extra!
- **Time:** 5–10 min · **Serves:** primes vision/ambition sessions.
- **Purpose:** Get everyone imagining success. Attendees write a future headline announcing the
  project's success ~1 year post-launch.
- **How to run:** 2 min silent writing → each person reads their headline aloud (1 line each).
- **Output:** A wall of "success headlines" that surface hopes and success criteria to revisit.
- **Notes:** Best before Elevator Pitch, Press Release, or goal-setting exercises.

### Two Truths & a Lie
- **Time:** 5–8 min · **Serves:** teaching board mechanics + rapport.
- **Purpose:** Warm the room and teach people to place/edit stickies before real work.
- **How to run:** Each person adds three stickies (two true, one false) about themselves; group guesses the lie.
- **Output:** Everyone has practised the core FigJam interactions. Discard after.
- **Notes:** Use with groups new to FigJam or new to each other.

### Either / Or
- **Time:** 5 min · **Serves:** fast engagement + a light read on preferences.
- **Purpose:** Quick dot-voting between paired options (e.g. "bold vs. understated", "playful vs. serious").
- **How to run:** Show 4–6 either/or pairs; everyone drops a dot on their pick; note the splits.
- **Output:** Early directional signal (useful before Art Direction / creative work).
- **Notes:** Also doubles as a board-mechanics teacher (teaches dot-voting).

## Context & Alignment

### Elevator Pitch
- **Time:** 15–20 min · **Serves:** align on what the product *is* and its value.
- **Purpose:** Force a crisp articulation of the offering, audience, and differentiator.
- **How to run:** Individuals fill the template *"For [audience] who [need], [product] is a
  [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator]."* → cluster → agree one.
- **Output:** A shared one-sentence positioning statement.
- **Notes:** Don't pair with Press Release (redundant). Great opener for brand/website projects.

### Press Release (working-backwards)
- **Time:** 20–30 min · **Serves:** align on the future vision and outcomes.
- **Purpose:** Describe the launched product as a news story to expose the intended impact.
- **How to run:** In pairs, draft a short launch press release (headline, sub-head, quote, key
  benefit) → read aloud → highlight recurring themes.
- **Output:** A vision narrative and implicit success criteria.
- **Notes:** Deeper alternative to Elevator Pitch; pick one, not both.

### Goals, Signals & Metrics
- **Time:** 20–25 min · **Serves:** define what success means and how it's measured.
- **Purpose:** Turn fuzzy ambitions into goals → observable signals → metrics.
- **How to run:** Brainstorm goals (silent) → group → for the top 3, add "how would we see it?"
  (signal) and "what would we measure?" (metric).
- **Output:** A goals→signals→metrics table feeding KPIs & Benchmarking.
- **Notes:** Strong when the brief lacks clear success criteria.

### Stakeholder & Landscape Map
- **Time:** 15–20 min · **Serves:** surface who/what shapes the project.
- **Purpose:** Map stakeholders (influence/interest) and known constraints/dependencies.
- **How to run:** Plot stakeholders on an influence×interest grid; list constraints in a side column.
- **Output:** A stakeholder map + constraints list to inform scope and interviews.
- **Notes:** Useful early on complex/enterprise engagements.

## Users

### Proto-Persona Sketch
- **Time:** 20–30 min · **Serves:** align on who we're building for (pre-research).
- **Purpose:** Capture the team's *current assumptions* about key audiences, to validate later.
- **How to run:** Small groups each take an audience and fill: goals, frustrations, context,
  "what would make this a win for them". Label everything **assumption to validate**.
- **Output:** 2–4 proto-personas (hypotheses) that feed the user-research plan.
- **Notes:** Only when real personas don't exist yet. Never present as validated fact.

### Empathy / "A Day in the Life"
- **Time:** 20–25 min · **Serves:** build shared empathy for the primary user.
- **Purpose:** Walk through a user's context, thoughts, and pain points across a scenario.
- **How to run:** Pick one audience; map Says / Thinks / Does / Feels, or a step-by-step day.
- **Output:** An empathy map / journey sketch highlighting pains and opportunities.
- **Notes:** Pairs well after a persona sketch; feeds journey mapping later.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done Statements
- **Time:** 20–25 min · **Serves:** reframe needs around motivation, not features.
- **Purpose:** Capture "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."
- **How to run:** Individuals draft JTBD statements → cluster → pick the highest-value jobs.
- **Output:** A prioritised set of jobs to design toward.
- **Notes:** Good bridge from Users into Ideation.

## Ideation & Design

### Crazy 8s / Rapid Sketching
- **Time:** 20–30 min · **Serves:** generate many divergent ideas fast.
- **Purpose:** Break fixation by producing 8 quick concepts each, then sharing.
- **How to run:** 8 sketches × 1 min (or 4 × 2 min for remote) → gallery walk → dot-vote favourites.
- **Output:** A field of concepts + early signal on which resonate.
- **Notes:** Diverge step; always follow with a converge/prioritise activity.

### Lightning Demos / Inspiration Wall
- **Time:** 15–20 min · **Serves:** build a shared reference vocabulary.
- **Purpose:** Collect examples (in and out of category) that do something well.
- **How to run:** Each person brings/links 1–2 examples and gives a 60-sec "what's good here".
- **Output:** An annotated inspiration wall for creative direction.
- **Notes:** Feeds Art Direction & Branding; pairs with Either/Or.

### How Might We (HMW)
- **Time:** 15–20 min · **Serves:** turn problems into solvable opportunity framings.
- **Purpose:** Reframe pains/insights as "How might we…?" prompts.
- **How to run:** From pains/insights on the board, rewrite each as an HMW → group → star the sharpest.
- **Output:** A set of HMW prompts to carry into design.
- **Notes:** A strong converge step and a natural synthesis tail.

## Prioritisation

### Value–Effort Matrix
- **Time:** 15–20 min · **Serves:** decide what to focus on.
- **Purpose:** Plot ideas/features by value (to user/business) vs. effort.
- **How to run:** Place items on the 2×2; discuss the "high value / low effort" quadrant first.
- **Output:** A ranked shortlist and an explicit "not now" pile.
- **Notes:** Default prioritisation tool. Alternative: Buy a Feature (below).

### Buy a Feature
- **Time:** 20–30 min · **Serves:** force trade-offs via constrained budgets.
- **Purpose:** Give each person a fixed "budget" to spend across priced features; scarcity forces choices.
- **How to run:** Price each feature, give budgets, let people (or groups) buy → debrief what got funded.
- **Output:** A funded feature set that reveals collective priorities and willingness to trade.
- **Notes:** More negotiation/energy than Value–Effort; needs a defined feature list. Pick one.

### Dot-Voting / Impact Ranking
- **Time:** 10 min · **Serves:** quick convergence on a long list.
- **Purpose:** Narrow many options to a few with a simple vote.
- **How to run:** Each person gets N dots; place on top choices; tally.
- **Output:** A ranked shortlist.
- **Notes:** Lightweight; use to close a divergent activity when time is tight.

## Reflection (optional, usually last)

### Rose / Bud / Thorn
- **Time:** 10–15 min · **Serves:** capture what's working, promising, and worrying.
- **Purpose:** Quick structured reflection on the project or the session.
- **How to run:** Everyone adds Roses (working), Buds (potential), Thorns (concerns); group briefly.
- **Output:** A sentiment snapshot + risks to carry into the Risk Radar / Discovery Readout.
- **Notes:** Nice closer that surfaces risks without a full retro.

---

## Choosing an off-list activity
The library isn't exhaustive. If no listed activity serves a stated goal, propose one anyway —
but **name it, give it a timebox, and state the gap it fills** in the agenda (e.g. "Assumptions
& Unknowns audit — 15 min — because the brief is thin and we need to surface what we don't
know"). Keep it in the same diverge→converge spirit and make sure it maps to a real goal.
