# Example Workshop Board (anonymized, structural exemplar)

A synthetic 90-minute discovery workshop for a **B2B/B2C website redesign**. Use it as a model
for board *shape and flow*, not for content — every cell here is a structure/prompt, never a
real answer.

**Session:** Discovery Workshop — [Client] Website Redesign · 90 min · remote (FigJam) ·
6 participants (product, marketing, ops, design lead + 2 facilitators).

**Goals:** (1) align on vision & success, (2) agree who the primary audiences are, (3) get an
early read on priorities.

---

## Board sections (left → right)

### 1. Onboarding zone
- Title card · Agenda (mirrors the run-of-show) · Figma basics · shortcuts · colour legend.

### 2. Warm-up — Extra, Extra! (8 min)
- Prompt text: *"It's one year post-launch. Write the headline announcing this project's success."*
- Empty per-person lanes for one headline each.

### 3. Elevator Pitch (20 min) — *serves goal 1*
- Template block: *"For [audience] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit].
  Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator]."*
- Empty area for individual drafts → an empty "agreed pitch" box.

### 4. Break (5 min)
- Simple timer note.

### 5. Proto-Persona Sketch (30 min) — *serves goal 2*
- Three empty audience cards, each with quadrants: Goals / Frustrations / Context / What's a win.
- Banner reminder: **"Assumptions to validate — not researched fact."**

### 6. Priorities — Dot-Voting (12 min) — *serves goal 3*
- Empty list frame ("candidate priorities") + note: "3 dots per person."

### 7. Reflection — Rose / Bud / Thorn (10 min)
- Three empty columns: Rose (working) / Bud (potential) / Thorn (concern).

### 8. Parking Lot
- One empty labelled frame for off-topic-but-important items.

### 9. Synthesis tail (for after the session)
- Three empty labelled frames: Opportunity Statements → Themes → How Might We.

---

## Why this flow works (the pattern in action)
- **Warm-up first**, then **diverge → converge**: generate a vision (Elevator Pitch) and
  audiences (personas) before narrowing (dot-vote).
- **Context → Users → Prioritise → Reflect** ordering.
- One warm-up + **two** longer exercises fits ~90 min with intro, a break, wrap, and buffer.
- The **synthesis tail** lets the raw output roll straight into insights (feeding
  `pdd-project-brief`, `pdd-archetypes`, or a discovery readout afterward).

Everything on the board is empty scaffolding and prompts — the participants fill it live.
