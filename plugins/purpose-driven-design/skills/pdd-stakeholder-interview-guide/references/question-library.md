# Stakeholder Interview Question Library

A reference bank of questions organised by section and role type. Always pick and adapt — never use all of them.
Every question can be followed up with "Why?", "Can you tell me more about that?", or "Do you have a recent example?"

---

## Warm-Up / Background (always include a few)

- Tell me about how you first became a part of [company].
- Tell us about your role at [company] — what's your day-to-day like?
- What would you say your goals are within the organization?
- How does [this project / this product / mental health / etc.] fit into your day-to-day work?
- How long have you been in this role?

---

## Project Context & Goals

- Why is now the right time to do this?
- What would success look like coming out of this effort — immediately?
- What about five years from now?
- Are there specific metrics you want to measure? What results do you want to see?
- In your opinion, what is the goal of this project? Why is it being started?
- What would a failed outcome look like to you?
- What keeps you up at night about this project?
- Do you think other teams are aligned on these goals, or do you expect some tension?
- **What's the one thing that, if we got it right, would make this project a success?**

---

## Users & Audience

- Who is the most important person this site / product needs to reach?
- What do you want them to feel when they land on it? Is this different for different audiences?
- Who are the secondary audiences we should support?
- What do you think are the goals and motivations of the people who will visit this site?
- What about their concerns or hesitations — what might prevent someone from taking action?
- What pain points are we solving for them?
- **What do you think is the most important information to show to users on the site? Is this different for different audiences?**
- What is the main action you want users to take when they visit the site?
- What message do you want users to take away?
- At what point in the journey do you lose the most prospective users — where do people fall off?
- How will this new [site / product / experience] serve users better than what exists today?
- Are there barriers or feedback you're hearing from existing users that you want to address?
- Is there anything you wish you knew more about your audience that research could uncover?
- What are the most common questions your support team is asked? What about the most common complaints?
- Do you have any stories you've heard about user frustrations or experiences that stand out?

---

## Role-Specific: Leadership / Executive (CEO, CCO, President)

- How would you describe the role [this product / this service] plays in the company's future?
- What is the primary growth opportunity — new customer acquisition, retention, or expansion?
- What does the pipeline look like for expansion, and should what we build reflect that from day one?
- Where are customers coming from today — search, referrals, word of mouth, paid advertising?
- **How significant is the B2B side of this business — partnerships, institutional relationships? How does the site need to serve them?**
- Are there specific services or areas that are higher priority from a revenue perspective?
- I was told that before this role you [background]. How has that experience shaped where you want to take this?

---

## Role-Specific: Clinical / VP of Product/Services

- What do you see as must-have features or content for the site?
- What features or content approaches should we definitely avoid?
- What are the meaningful differences across [geographies / audiences / use cases] that should be reflected?
- What are some common misconceptions your audience might have about [this space]?
- What's the thing that most often gets in the way of a customer following through?
- What's something we can do to build greater trust with prospective customers on the website?
- **What gaps does [company] specifically address that most competitors don't?**
- Do you know of any data or statistics on effectiveness that could be featured on the site?

---

## Role-Specific: Marketing / Growth

- What channels are you currently using to market this and how are they performing?
- Tell me more about your goals when it comes to SEO — what terms do you want to rank for?
- What role do you see content (blog, articles, resources) playing in the future?
- How much throughline in content and branding are you anticipating with the main brand?
- How frequently do you anticipate needing to update content?
- How prominent do you want specific team members, providers, or individual bios to be?
- What's your current process for [channel] — what's working and what isn't?

---

## Role-Specific: Operations / Technology

- Are there technology decisions already made that will directly shape or constrain what this [site / product] can do?
- What systems and integrations need to be considered — scheduling, patient portal, billing, CRM?
- Is there any possibility of [feature X — e.g. insurance eligibility check, live chat, referral form]? What would it require?
- What does the current [booking / onboarding / intake] flow look like, and is there room for improvement?
- What do you think of the current [specific flow]? What works and what doesn't?
- Who will own the [site / product] day-to-day once it's live — content updates, technical maintenance?
- What are your feelings on [CMS / platform]? How comfortable is the team with it?
- Does the experience need to support multiple languages?
- Are there compliance or security constraints we should know about?

---

## Role-Specific: Business Development / Sales

- **At what point in the journey do you lose the most prospective customers — where do people fall off?**
- Where are patients / customers coming from today?
- Is there anything about [company / product] that you find yourself having to explain or sell that should be immediately obvious from the website?
- What makes [company] credible to a B2B partner — is it outcomes data, credentials, scale, brand, or something else?
- Is there anything the website can do better to support business development conversations?
- I heard you've worked at [previous company]. What learnings are you bringing from that experience?

---

## Content

- What do you think of the content on the [current site / existing product] today?
- What is working well and what could be improved?
- What areas of the site's content or services do you want to expand on?
- What content governance or approval workflows need to be built into how the CMS is set up?
- Are there meaningful differences across [geographies / customer segments] that should be reflected in the content?
- Who is responsible for adding and editing content on the site once it's live?

---

## Competitors & Differentiation

- What do you see other companies out in the world doing that you like and want to emulate?
- What do you see other companies doing that you want to avoid?
- **What do you think competitors are getting wrong that you have an opportunity to get right?**
- Who are your product's biggest competitors?
- What makes your product stand out from the competition?
- Is there a company outside of [your industry] — in any industry — that you think has built a brand you admire and want to learn from?
- How will you differentiate yourself and stand out from other similar offerings out there?

---

## Risk & Concerns

- What keeps you up at night about this project or this launch?
- What would a failed launch look like to you?
- Anything that concerns you about this project or that you think could be a risk?
- Do you think other teams are aligned on these project goals, or do you expect some conflict?

---

## Ideas & Vision

- If you could add any feature or content to the site regardless of time or budget, what would that be and why?
- What products or experiences out in the world have a similar look and feel that you want to achieve with this?
- Are there any specific brand guidelines we should be aware of?
- If you had a magic wand and could instantly change one thing, what would it be?
- Is there anything else you wish the product could do that it currently can't?

---

## Wrap-Up (always end with these)

- Is there anything else you think we should know that we haven't covered?
- Is there anyone else you think we should talk to?

---

## Probing / Follow-Up Prompts (use freely throughout)

- Can you tell me a bit more about that?
- Do you have a recent example?
- How did that experience shape your thinking?
- What else? Are there other examples?
- Why is that important to you?
- What would that look like in practice?
- What would need to be true for that to happen?
