## Vet Med Student

**1st-Person Narrative:**
I am just starting on my path to become a veterinarian, something I've wanted to do for as long as I can remember! Studying full time at school and my work as a tech in a small clinic keeps me really busy. From what I've learned about the industry I can tell it's going to be a tough road ahead with the potential for burnout, which makes me nervous, but I'm hoping I'll be prepared. I am always looking for new sources of info I can use to learn and improve, like podcasts to listen on my commute or testing my knowledge with CE courses. I'm glad I have a supportive community at my school to help me figure this all out, because it can be overwhelming.

> ✏️ Note: The narrative is written in a warm, personal voice. It captures emotions (nervous, hopeful), daily life (commute, school + work), and orientation toward the product/service (seeking content, community, resources).

### Goals

- Graduate school and become a veterinarian
- Become part of the industry community with support from peers and mentorship from experienced veterinarians
- Make a difference in the lives of animals
- Improve the industry with fresh new perspectives on mental health and diversity, equity, and inclusion

> ✏️ Note: Goals reflect aspirations, not just immediate tasks. They go beyond the product/service context.

---

### Frustrations

- Keeping up with the volume of information being learned in school
- Translating jargon and medical terminology they aren't yet familiar with
- Balancing life between school and work
- Preparing for the challenges of burnout and compassion fatigue ahead
- Dealing with the brunt of client frustrations as a lower-level employee in clinics
- Experiencing the negative sides of vet med, like euthanasia, for the first time

> ✏️ Note: Frustrations are specific and behavioral — not vague sentiments like "overwhelmed." Each should be traceable to interview data.

---

### Needs

- Easy to find, accurate, and up-to-date content to help reinforce what they are learning in school
- A supportive community that understands the struggles unique to the vet world
- Mental health resources and support from professionals
- Resources to help seek career opportunities

> ✏️ Note: Needs are framed as product/service requirements — what the product must deliver to serve this archetype.

---

### Habits

- Doesn't yet need CE credits, but likes to take quizzes and courses to reinforce learning
- Typically finds content online via Google search and search platforms on websites
- Tends to bookmark and save articles for later reference
- Finds out about new resources through professors

> ✏️ Note: Habits describe observable behaviors — how they find, consume, and act on information. Avoid interpreting motivation here; stick to the behavior.
